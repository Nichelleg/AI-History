## About This Repository

This repository documents my coursework, research assignments,
collaborative projects, and reflections from History of Artificial
Intelligence at Houston City College.

The course explored the historical development of artificial
intelligence, from its earliest conceptual foundations to the
technologies and applications shaping modern AI.

Studying the history of AI provided valuable context for
understanding how advances in mathematics, computer science,
machine learning, and computing infrastructure contributed
to the development of today's intelligent systems.

This repository serves as both an academic record and part
of my professional AI & Robotics portfolio.

## Personal Reflection

One of the most valuable aspects of studying AI history
was developing a broader perspective on the field.

Modern artificial intelligence is often presented as
a sudden technological breakthrough. However, its
development reflects decades of research, experimentation,
technical limitations, and scientific progress.

Understanding this history provides context for evaluating
both the capabilities and limitations of emerging AI systems.

The collaborative midterm project also provided an
opportunity to apply leadership and organizational skills
within a technical academic environment.

These experiences reinforced the importance of combining
technical knowledge with research, critical thinking,
communication, and effective collaboration.
